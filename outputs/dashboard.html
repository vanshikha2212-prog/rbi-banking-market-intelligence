CAR is a benchmark-relative movement

Cumulative abnormal return adds daily bank returns after subtracting the market-model expected return. It is not the stock’s raw return or a trading profit.

Different horizons, different questions

GARCH estimates one-day conditional volatility using earlier observations. Absolute CAR describes the realised event-window movement. Their association does not establish forecasting skill.

Volatility categories are relative

Where supplied, Low/Medium/High/Very High refer to training-period volatility thresholds. They are not loss probabilities, credit ratings, or investment recommendations.
